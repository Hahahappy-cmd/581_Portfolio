A Family of Buttons: Four-Part Harmony

Four-Part Harmony

Each teammate's personality influences how they play, while their instrument is inspired by something they enjoy. Turn your sound on.

  1. 1Tap a person to wake them up. They start playing right away.
  2. 2Tap more people. Teammates with more similar profiles tend to synchronize more easily. Less similar teammates may begin at different rhythms, creating contrast.
  3. 3Drag people closer together to help them synchronize.
